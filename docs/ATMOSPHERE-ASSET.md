# Garden asset provenance

Created for Atmos with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool on 9 November 2026. No third-party stock photograph or runtime hotlink is used. The original generated image was converted to local WebP delivery assets with Sharp (1920px desktop, 960px mobile). The garden is illustrative, not a live camera view.

Delivered assets:

- `public/atmosphere/garden-mobile.webp`

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full-screen atmospheric garden background for an existing smart-home dashboard, NOT a UI mockup. Create one wide landscape 16:9 photographic scene, a peaceful private European garden seen from indoors, low lush grass filling the lower third, graceful leafy shrubs and trees along both sides, a soft foliage backdrop with a pale open patch of sky at the upper center. A few small white hydrangea flowers near the bottom right edge and a narrow subtle garden path at far left. Soft natural overcast daylight, muted sage/forest greens, beautiful real botanical texture, gentle photographic depth of field, dreamy slightly defocused garden, calm and premium, low contrast and generous uncluttered space in the center. Camera at window height. The entire output is scenery edge to edge. No window frame, no glass droplets, no text, no UI, no cards, no symbols, no logos, no people, no houses dominating the view, no artificial glow. Natural lighting will be adjusted later in CSS for weather and night, so provide neutral diffused daylight and retain detail in shadows.
